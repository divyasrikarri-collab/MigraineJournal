Privacy Policy

Migraine Journal for Android · Last updated 28 August 2026

Migraine Journal does not collect your data. Everything you log stays in a database on your phone. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. It does not request internet access, so it is not technically capable of transmitting your health information anywhere.

What the app stores, and where

All of the following is written to a private database on your device (migraine_journal.db) and to the app's local settings store. None of it is transmitted off the device by the app.

  • Migraine records — start and end time, pain level, pain locations and type, aura, symptoms, medication name, dose, time taken and how effective you rated it, and any notes you write.
  • Daily check-ins — sleep hours and quality, water intake, stress level, exercise, and optionally screen time, barometric pressure, and menstrual cycle day. Cycle tracking is off by default and only records anything if you switch it on.
  • Food entries — foods and drinks you log against a date, with the time.
  • Your settings — reminder on/off and time, preferred volume unit (ml or oz), and whether cycle tracking is enabled.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or profile.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, telemetry, or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party services receive your data, because no data is sent anywhere.
  • No location data is requested or accessed.
  • Your data is never sold or shared. There is no mechanism by which it could be.

Backups

Automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for this app. Your migraine history is not copied to Google Drive or moved to a new phone during setup. The trade-off is deliberate: health data does not leave the device without your say-so. If you change phones, use the export feature first.

Exporting your data

Settings offers a CSV export and a PDF summary intended for a doctor's appointment. Export files are written to storage private to this app and are then offered to Android's share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose a destination — an email, a messaging app, a cloud drive. Once you send a file to another app or service, this policy no longer covers it; the receiving service's own privacy policy applies. Files you never share stay on your device.

Permissions

The app requests one permission: notifications, used solely to post the optional daily check-in reminder at the time you choose. Declining it disables the reminder and affects nothing else. There is no internet permission.

Deleting your data

Settings → Clear all data permanently erases every migraine record, daily log, and food entry on the device. Uninstalling the app also removes the database and all settings. Because nothing is stored off the device, there is nothing left elsewhere to request deletion of.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling ever changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. Any change that would involve transmitting data off the device would be announced in the app before it took effect.
